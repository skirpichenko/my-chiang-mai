Privacy policy

My Chiang Mai · last updated November 15, 2026

My Chiang Mai is a blog about life in Chiang Mai, Thailand. It is published as the Telegram channels @my_chiangmai (Russian) and @my_chiangmai_en (English), the Facebook Page "My Chiang Mai", and this catalog site. This page explains what personal data these services handle. In short: we don't collect any.

The Facebook app

The Meta app "My Chiang Mai" is used only by the Page's own administrators. It publishes the blog's posts, with their photos and videos, to the Facebook Page, and edits or removes those posts.

  • It does not offer Facebook Login to visitors and does not ask anyone else for permissions.
  • It does not read, store or process data about people who view, like, comment on or message the Page.
  • Its access token is kept privately by the administrators and is used for nothing but publishing to the Page.

The catalog site

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

  • Searches and filters run in your browser and are not sent anywhere.
  • When the site is opened inside Telegram, it uses Telegram's Mini App script only to match Telegram's colors and window. It does not read or send your Telegram account data.
  • A post you open on the site is shown through Telegram's public post preview, which Telegram serves under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub, which hosts the site, may log technical request data such as IP addresses, under the GitHub privacy statement.

The Telegram channels

The channels are ordinary public Telegram channels. Telegram, not us, handles your account and subscription under the Telegram privacy policy. Our bot only copies the channel's own posts between the Russian and English channels.

Data deletion

Because none of these services collects or stores personal data about visitors or users, there is no personal data of yours for us to delete. If you believe we hold something about you, or you want a post that mentions you or your business changed or removed, send a message to the Facebook Page "My Chiang Mai" or to the Telegram channel @my_chiangmai, and we will deal with it promptly.

Changes

If the services start handling personal data, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
